## 🎮 About the Game

Doloc Town is a side-scrolling pixel-art farming simulation game with adventure, RPG, and casual elements. Developed by RedSaw Games Studio, it launched on August 5, 2026. Players gather resources, build a vertical farm, explore a post-apocalyptic world, fish, cook, ranch, craft, befriend townsfolk, modify crop genes, and fight enemies with a combat drone.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Doloc Town**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and progression controls for Doloc Town. It can adjust player survival resources, farming progress, inventory quantities, currency, drone combat performance, and world time while preserving the game's core farming, exploration, crafting, automation, and combat systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to reduce resource grinding, experiment with vertical farm layouts, speed up crop and crafting progression, explore the wasteland more freely, or make bullet-hell drone combat more accessible.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents the player from losing health during exploration and enemy encounters.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Stops stamina from decreasing while using tools, fishing, moving, or performing other energy-consuming activities.
- **Instant Crop Growth** — Advances planted crops toward harvest without waiting through the normal growth period.
- **Instant Crafting** — Completes eligible crafting and processing actions without their normal waiting time.
- **Freeze World Time** — Pauses the in-game clock while allowing the player to continue exploring, farming, crafting, and managing the settlement.
- **Automation Power Lock** — Keeps eligible farming and processing automation supplied with the resources needed to continue operating.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Drone Energy** — Prevents the combat drone's energy supply from being depleted during battles.
- **Damage Multiplier** — Increases the damage dealt by the combat drone against enemies.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Allows equipped drone weapons to defeat regular enemies with a single successful attack.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Add Currency** — Adds currency for purchasing tools, upgrades, materials, equipment, and farm improvements.
- **Item Quantity Editor** — Adjusts the quantity of a selected stack of resources, crops, fish, food, materials, or crafting components.
- **No Item Consumption** — Prevents selected consumable items and crafting materials from being reduced when used.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Doloc Town uses side-scrolling platform movement across farms, settlements, caves, wetlands, mining areas, forests, river valleys, and the Old City Ruins. Use Unlimited Stamina when repeated tool use, fishing, exploration, or evasive movement becomes restrictive. Freeze World Time is useful when exploring large areas or completing several activities during one in-game day.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory before using Add Currency or Item Quantity Editor. Select a stack only after the inventory interface has fully loaded, then apply the desired quantity and close the menu normally. Use No Item Consumption for food, materials, and other selected items when testing recipes or preparing large construction projects. Keep quest objects and unique progression items unchanged unless a backup save is available.

### World & Environment

The game connects resource gathering, farming, crafting, weather management, ranching, fishing, relationships, and settlement development. Instant Crop Growth helps test crop layouts and gene-modified seeds, while Instant Crafting speeds up processing and construction. Automation Power Lock supports farms that rely on agricultural, collection, nurturing, processing, and other drone-based production systems.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on a customizable drone equipped with weapons and support modules. The player focuses on movement, dodging, positioning, and survival while the drone attacks enemies. Unlimited Drone Energy keeps the combat system active, Damage Multiplier shortens encounters, and One-Hit Defeat is best reserved for clearing ordinary enemies quickly. Disable combat options before story events, boss transitions, or scripted encounters.
